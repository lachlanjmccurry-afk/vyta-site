Distress indicators

How much financial trouble is the business in right now? Higher is worse.

Distress indicators
IDIndicator and what it checksWarning levelsWeight
D1Current ratioWhether short-term assets (cash, money owed by customers, stock) cover the debts due within a year.Amber 1.20 or belowRed 1.00 or below8
D2Quick ratioThe same test using only cash and money owed by customers, the things that turn into cash quickly.Amber 1.00 or belowRed 0.70 or below8
D3Cash runwayHow many months the cash would last at the recent rate it has been going out.Amber 6 months or belowRed 3 months or below10
D4ATO debt risingHow many months in a row the amount owed to the ATO has grown.Amber 2 months running or moreRed 3 months running or more12
D5Bank debt growthHow much bank loans and overdraft have grown over the last six months.Amber 10% or aboveRed 25% or above7
D6Overdraft useHow much of the approved overdraft limit is being used. Needs the limit from the owner.Amber 85% or aboveRed 100% or above6
D7Creditor daysHow long, on average, suppliers are waiting to be paid.Amber 60 days or aboveRed 90 days or above7
D8Creditors 90+ daysThe share of unpaid supplier bills that are more than 90 days overdue.Amber 15% or aboveRed 30% or above7
D9Debtor days trendWhether customers are taking longer to pay than they were three months ago.Amber +10 days or moreRed +20 days or more5
D10Debtors 90+ daysThe share of money owed by customers that is more than 90 days overdue.Amber 15% or aboveRed 25% or above5
D11Sales up, cash downMonths in a row where sales rose but cash fell: growth that is eating cash.Amber 2 months running or moreRed 3 months running or more8
D12Cash flow ratioWhether a year of trading produced enough cash to cover short-term debts.Amber 0.40 or belowRed 0.20 or below7
D13Debt to assetsTotal debts as a share of everything the business owns.Amber 0.70 or aboveRed 1.00 or above10

Viability indicators

If the cash problem were fixed, would the business make money? Higher is better.

Viability indicators
IDIndicator and what it checksWarning levelsWeight
V1Equity ratioThe owners' stake (net assets) as a share of everything the business owns.Amber 0.20 or belowRed 0.00 or below15
V2Gross marginWhat is left from each dollar of sales after the direct cost of making the sale.Amber 30% or belowRed 15% or below15
V3Margin trendWhether gross margin is improving or slipping compared with the year before. Needs 24 months of data.Amber −2.0 pts or belowRed −5.0 pts or below10
V4Overheads vs GPHow much of the gross profit the running costs use up.Amber 90% or aboveRed 100% or above15
V5Underlying marginProfit before interest, depreciation and one-off costs, as a share of sales. The core test of viability.Amber 5% or belowRed 0% or below20
V6Revenue trendSales over the last 12 months compared with the 12 months before.Amber -5% or belowRed -15% or below10
V7Pipeline coverOpen quotes compared with average monthly sales. Needs quotes data.Amber 1.00 or belowRed 0.50 or below5
V8Long-term coverLong-term assets compared with long-term debts.Amber 1.20 or belowRed 1.00 or below10
