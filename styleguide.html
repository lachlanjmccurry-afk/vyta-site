Vyta design system

The building blocks every page of the website is made from. It uses the same colours, type and shapes as the dashboard, so the website and the product feel like one thing. Try the menus in the header above, then make the window narrow to see the phone layout.

Colour

Navy and white do most of the work, and teal is the one accent. Red, amber and green only ever show results, the same as in the dashboard, so a colour always means something.

Drawn product screen

The dashboard’s Overview page, drawn in code rather than as a screenshot, so it’s sharp on every screen and easy to update. The figures are the made-up “Stressed Co” test business, scored by the real engine. Feature pages will use the same approach for Details, Cash, What if? and the rest.

Feature items

For lists of benefits: an icon tile, a short title and one or two sentences. They sit on a thin rule instead of in boxes, so the page doesn’t turn into a wall of cards.

Two honest scores

Distress shows how much trouble is building. Viability shows whether the business underneath still works.

Every reason shown

Each of the 21 indicators shows the figures it used and a 12-month trend. Nothing is a black box.
