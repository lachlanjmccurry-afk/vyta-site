Product History

Not a one-off check. A daily watch.

Vyta refreshes from Xero every morning and saves both scores and every indicator’s result. Over time, the direction matters as much as today’s number.

Both scores, over time

One chart per score, because they point in opposite directions.

Distress going up is bad; viability going up is good. Keeping them apart makes the trend impossible to misread.
