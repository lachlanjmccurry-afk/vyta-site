The restructure candidate

High distress, strong viability: the business worth saving.

Vyta scores distress and viability separately. When a business is in serious financial trouble but the model underneath still makes money, Vyta flags it as a Restructure candidate: a profitable business with a legacy balance-sheet problem.

Those are the cases where small business restructuring, voluntary administration or a safe harbour plan can work, if they start in time.

How referrals work

Matched, not sold.

Listed practitioners appear on a business’s Get help page when its pathway and results fit the work they do. The business sees why you were matched, and chooses whether to get in touch.

  • ASIC register check. Registered liquidators are checked against ASIC’s register before listing.
  • Flat fee, no commission. Vyta takes nothing on appointments.
  • No data shared without consent. Vyta never sends a business’s data or scores to an adviser; the owner decides what to share.
  • Matched by state. Businesses see advisers in their own state first.
