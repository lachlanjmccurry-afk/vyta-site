Security & your data

Vyta can look at your books. It can’t touch them.

Your financial data is sensitive, and a tool that judges your business’s health has to earn trust. Here’s exactly what Vyta can and can’t do.

Read-only, by design

Vyta asks Xero only for read permissions. It has no permission to create invoices, post journals, make payments or change settings, so it can’t, even by mistake.

You sign in with Xero

You connect through Xero’s own sign-in page and approve the access there. Vyta never sees or stores your Xero password.

Disconnect any time

Remove Vyta from your Xero account’s connected apps whenever you like. Its access stops straight away.

Your results

Your scores are yours.

Vyta doesn’t send your data or scores to advisers, lenders or anyone else. When Vyta suggests an adviser, they learn nothing about you unless you contact them, and you decide what to share.

  • No surprises in your books. Vyta never writes to Xero.
  • No selling data. Adviser listings are paid for with a flat fee, not with your information.
  • A safe demo. The public demo runs only on two made-up businesses. It never connects to Xero and nothing you do in it is saved.

Questions

Security questions

Can Vyta move money or change my books?

No. Vyta only holds read permissions, so Xero won’t let it create, edit or delete anything, including payments.

How do I disconnect Vyta?

In Xero, open your settings and go to connected apps, then disconnect Vyta. Its access ends straight away.

Does my adviser see my scores?

Only if you share them. Vyta doesn’t send your data or scores to anyone. You can export a PDF report and share it yourself.
