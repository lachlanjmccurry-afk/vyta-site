How it works

Three steps, about ten minutes.

  1. 1

    Connect Xero

    Sign in to Xero and give Vyta permission to read your books. It can look, but it can’t touch: nothing is ever changed or sent from your file.

  2. 2

    Vyta reads the last twelve months

    It checks the same warning signs an insolvency practitioner or the ATO would look for: cash running short, tax debts piling up, bills going unpaid, customers paying late.

  3. 3

    You get two honest answers

    How much trouble the business is in, and whether it can recover. Each comes with the reasons behind it, the three things to deal with first, and what’s already going well.

For business owners

You know something’s not right. Vyta tells you what, and how serious it is.

Most owners who end up in real trouble saw it coming. They just couldn’t tell whether a bad quarter was a rough patch or the start of something worse, and by the time the accountant looked, the options had narrowed.

Vyta gives you that answer now, while there’s still time to act on it.

  • A straight answer

    Not a wall of ratios. A short summary of where you stand and why, written the way a good adviser would say it across the table.

  • What to fix first

    The three issues doing the most damage, so you know where to spend your energy this week.

  • Room to try things

    See what happens if you raise prices, cut costs, chase debtors faster or set up a payment plan with the ATO, before you commit to any of it.

  • Something to bring to your accountant

    Export a clear report so the conversation starts from the facts, not from a shoebox of statements.

For accounting and restructuring firms

Find the businesses that need you, early.

By the time most clients ask for help, the cash is gone and the choices are few. The signs were usually sitting in their Xero file months earlier.

Vyta reads those signs across your client list, so you can pick up the phone while a turnaround is still possible, and tell the difference between a client worth restructuring and one that needs a different conversation.

  • See who’s slipping

    Every connected client scored for distress, so the ones heading for trouble rise to the top of the list.

  • Know who can be saved

    A separate viability score shows whether the underlying business still works, which helps you choose between a turnaround, a safe harbour plan or a wind-down.

  • Grounded in the indicators you already use

    Built around the ASIC v Plymin indicators and ATO rules, with every result showing the figures behind it. Nothing is a black box.

  • A better first meeting

    Walk in with the cash story, the tax position and the top issues already laid out, and spend the time on the plan.
