How it works

Set up once. Then it keeps watch.

No spreadsheets to fill in and no reports to request. Vyta works from what's already in Xero.

  1. 1

    Connect Xero

    Sign in with Xero and pick your business. Vyta asks for read-only access, so it can look but never touch.

  2. 2

    Vyta runs the checks

    Every morning at 7am it pulls the latest figures and re-scores the business against all 23 checks.

  3. 3

    You know where you stand

    Two scores, your top three issues, what's working, and advisers who can help with your situation. Export it as a PDF for your accountant.

What it watches

The warning signs people miss until it's late.

Vyta keeps an eye on 23 warning signs across six areas of the business, and tells you in plain English which ones need attention.

$

Cash

How much is in the bank, and how long it will last at the current pace.

%

Tax and super

ATO balances, GST, PAYG and super that are quietly building up.

⇄

Suppliers and customers

Bills being paid later and later, and invoices customers aren't paying.

↗

Loans and the overdraft creeping up month after month.

±

Whether each sale still makes money once the overheads are paid.

◷

Sales dropping away from a normal month, and books falling behind.
