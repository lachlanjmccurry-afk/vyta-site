Why it matters

The warning signs show up months before the crisis.

Most businesses that fail send signals long before the end: tax debts creeping up, suppliers waiting longer, cash slipping month after month. They’re all in the Xero file. They just don’t add up to an answer on their own.

Here’s a made-up business, scored by Vyta at the end of each month:

  1. Oct 2025Distress 33. Already a “Turnaround review”, with 4 red warning signs. Plenty of options.
  2. Jan 2026Distress 63 and High. The bank account is overdrawn and the ATO balance has risen three months running.
  3. Sep 2026Distress 89. $30,000 overdrawn, $68,455 owed to the ATO, suppliers waiting 99 days.

Seeing it in October rather than September is the difference between a turnaround and a liquidation.

Built to be trusted

Grounded in the tests the courts and the ATO use.

Vyta isn’t a black box. Its indicators come from the checklist Australian courts use to judge insolvency, ASIC v Plymin, plus the ATO debts that matter most to directors. Every result shows the figures behind it.

Security and your data

  • Read-only access. Vyta asks Xero for read permissions only. It can’t create, change or delete anything, and you can disconnect it from Xero at any time.
  • Every number explained. Each result shows the calculation, the figures it used and where the warning levels sit.
  • Honest about gaps. If Vyta doesn’t have enough data for a check, it says “Not assessed” and leaves it out, rather than guessing. Every result carries a data confidence rating.
  • Your results stay yours. Vyta never sends your data or scores to an adviser. You decide who to contact and what to share.
Questions

Common questions

What do I need to use Vyta?

A Xero subscription with your business’s books in it, ideally with at least 12 months of trading. Vyta works with fewer months too, but tells you its confidence is lower.

Will Vyta change anything in my Xero file?

No. Vyta only asks Xero for read permissions, so it can’t create invoices, post journals or change settings. You can remove its access at any time from Xero’s connected apps settings.

Is this financial or legal advice?

No. Vyta gives indicative early-warning signals based on your Xero data. It isn’t an insolvency determination or professional advice. If the results worry you, speak to a qualified accountant, adviser or registered liquidator. Vyta can help you find one.

How often does it update?

Vyta refreshes from Xero every day and saves each result, so you can see how the scores move over time and which indicators have changed colour.
