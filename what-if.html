Product What if?

Test a plan before you commit to it.

Move a few sliders and Vyta re-scores the business on the spot, with exactly the same rules as the real scores. Nothing you try changes your data.

The levers

Six things you can change

Prices

Raise prices by a percentage and see the extra gross profit flow through.

Sales volume

Model growth, or test what a drop in sales would do.

Cost of sales

See what a better supplier deal or less waste would be worth.

Overhead

Try trimming rent, wages or other running costs by a percentage.

Debtor collection

Collect from customers a set number of days faster and turn it into cash.

ATO payment plan

Put the ATO debt on a 12-month plan and see what it does to the scores.

An example

From “Likely liquidation” to “Turnaround review”

For the made-up Stressed Co, Vyta wrote this after four changes were tried together:

Raising prices by 10%, cutting overhead by 20%, collecting debtors 30 days faster and putting the ATO debt on a 12-month payment plan moves this business from High to Moderate distress and from Weak to Uncertain viability.

It’s a model, not a promise. It shows which levers matter most, so you can take a realistic plan to an adviser.

Good to know

How the model works

Is the what-if score worked out differently?

No. Vyta adjusts a copy of your figures and runs it through exactly the same scoring rules as the real result. With every slider at zero, you get exactly your real scores.

What does it assume?

Price, volume, cost and overhead changes are applied to each of the last 12 months, and the extra profit is assumed to arrive as cash. Faster collection turns that many days of sales from money owed into cash. The ATO plan pays the first of 12 instalments from cash.

Does trying a scenario change my real scores?

No. Nothing on the What if? page changes your data, your Xero file or your real scores.
