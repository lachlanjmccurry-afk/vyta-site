For business owners

You know something’s not right. Vyta tells you what, and how serious it is.

Most owners who end up in real trouble saw it coming. They just couldn’t tell whether a bad quarter was a rough patch or the start of something worse. Vyta gives you that answer while there’s still time to act on it.

The questions that keep owners up at night

  • “Is this just a bad patch?” Vyta looks at twelve months, not one, and shows which way each sign is heading.
  • “Where did the money go?” The cash waterfall walks from last year’s balance to today’s, step by step.
  • “How worried should I be about the ATO?” Vyta tracks how long the ATO balance has been growing, and explains why it matters for directors.
  • “Is the business actually any good?” The Viability score looks past the cash squeeze to the margins and overheads underneath.
  • “What should I do first?” The top three issues, ranked by how much damage they’re doing.
  • “Who should I even call?” Advisers matched to your situation, with the reason each one fits.
A straight answer

Not a wall of ratios.

A one-line verdict, two scores and a short list of what to deal with first, written the way a good adviser would say it across the table. Every number links to the reason behind it.

What you need

If your books are in Xero, you’re ready.

Vyta works best with twelve months of trading in Xero. With less, it still runs, and tells you its confidence is lower.

You don’t need to be good with numbers. That’s the point.

  • About ten minutes to set up. Sign in to Xero, approve read-only access, and your first result appears.
  • Nothing to maintain. Vyta refreshes from Xero every day on its own.
  • Your books stay untouched. Vyta can only read. It never creates, changes or deletes anything.
  • Your results stay yours. Nothing is shared with an adviser unless you choose to share it.
